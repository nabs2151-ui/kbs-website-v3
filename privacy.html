Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through our store, we collect certain information from you, including your name, delivery address, phone number, and email address. This is collectively referred to as "Order Information."

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the Order Information that we collect generally to fulfill any orders placed through the site. This includes:

  • Arranging for shipping and providing you with tracking updates.
  • Communicating with you to verify your Cash on Delivery (COD) order.
  • Providing customer support regarding your purchase.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share your Order Information with trusted third-party courier services (e.g., TCS, Leopards, CallCourier) strictly for the purpose of delivering your package to your doorstep.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through the site, we will maintain your Order Information for our records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.
